# Claims and Constraints

This file defines claim boundaries for generated InfoClub presentations.
The presentation generator must use this file to avoid unsupported claims.

## Allowed Claims

- InfoClub presentations may describe only facts present in approved knowledge files.
- Unknown fields may be presented as items requiring confirmation, not as facts.

## Unknown Facts

- Organization mission.
- Organization vision.
- Community type.
- Primary activities.
- Core benefits.
- Services.
- Events.
- Audience goals, pain points, and expected value.
- Evidence for value propositions.

## Prohibited Assumptions

- Do not invent metrics, traction, revenue, member counts, partner names, sponsor names, or investor interest.
- Do not claim specific outcomes unless they are supported by verified evidence.
- Do not infer InfoClub services or events from generic community patterns.
- Do not present unverified audience pain points as known facts.

## Verification Required

- Any public factual claim about InfoClub.
- Any quantitative claim.
- Any claim about partners, investors, sponsors, members, services, or events.
- Any brand, legal, financial, or operational statement intended for external presentation.
