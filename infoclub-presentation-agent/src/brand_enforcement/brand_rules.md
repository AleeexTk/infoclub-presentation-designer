# Brand Enforcement Rules

## Required Brand Sources

- `brand/brand_guide.md`
- `brand/colors.md`
- `brand/typography.md`
- `brand/presentation_rules.md`

## Enforcement Rules

- Do not remove verification markers from presentation content.
- Do not add new factual claims while applying brand rules.
- Keep slide structure clear and presentation-ready.
- Align tone and formatting with available brand guidance.
- If a brand source contains unknown or placeholder values, mark the brand decision as verification required.

## Output Rules

- The output must be a branded presentation draft.
- Each slide should preserve its title and content unless brand rules require presentation-safe wording.
- Each slide should record which brand rule groups were applied.
- Rendering-specific decisions are out of scope until an artifact renderer exists.
